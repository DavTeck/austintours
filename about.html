Our Story

About Austins Tour & Travel

Crafting extraordinary journeys with heart, expertise, and a deep respect for the places we visit.

Born from a love of discovery

Founded in Uganda, Austins Tour & Travel is a passionate team of local experts dedicated to creating thrilling East African safari memories that last a lifetime.

Today we specialize in Uganda’s national parks and the wider East African region, partnering with trusted lodges, local guides, and conservation projects.

Every itinerary is built around you — your pace, your interests, your sense of wonder.

Our Values

These principles guide every decision we make.

Sustainability First

We prioritize low-impact travel, carbon-conscious itineraries, and partners who actively protect the environments and communities we visit. A portion of every booking supports local conservation.

Authentic Experiences

We believe the most memorable moments happen when you connect with people and place. We open doors to genuine cultural exchanges, not staged performances.
